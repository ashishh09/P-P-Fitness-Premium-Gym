About P&P Fitness

MORE THAN JUST A GYM

P&P Fitness is a fitness destination focused on helping individuals improve their strength, fitness and confidence through training, supportive guidance and a motivating environment.

Modern Equipment

Engineered with Jerai Fitness machines for smooth biomechanics, muscle isolation, and safe heavy lifting.

Spacious Workout Environment

Expansive floors with floor-to-ceiling glass panoramic views, open-air functional turf, and optimal climate control.

Supportive Trainers

Knowledgeable mentors on the floor dedicated to proper lifting technique, posture correction, and constant motivation.

Personalised Fitness Guidance

Structured workout regimens and realistic dietary consultation tailored directly to your individual targets.

Clean and Well-Maintained Facilities

Sanitized equipment, clean rubber flooring, modern washrooms, and hygienic workout stations maintained every single day.

Facilities & Amenities

BUILT FOR YOUR BEST WORKOUT

From heavy iron strength zones to relaxing post-workout wellness, experience comprehensive fitness facilities tailored to your health and transformation.

Modern Gym Equipment

Top-grade Jerai Fitness machines, pin-loaded selectors, multi-cable stations, and ergonomic plate-loaded presses for seamless lifting.

Strength Training

Olympic barbells, heavy-duty squat racks, flat/incline benches, calibrated iron plates, and a complete dumbbell series for progressive overload.

Personal Training

One-on-one dedicated training sessions with attentive coaching on proper execution, movement mechanics, and consistent progress tracking.

Diet & Fitness Guidance

Nutritional roadmaps, macro consultation, healthy habit development, and recovery protocols built specifically around your lifestyle.

Sauna

Thermal therapy designed to soothe strained muscles, improve circulation, promote relaxation, and accelerate post-workout recovery.

Swimming Pool

Low-impact cardiovascular conditioning, endurance laps, and therapeutic water training for complete, joint-friendly wellness.

Online Fitness Classes

Remote workout programming and virtual fitness guidance to keep you disciplined and moving even when travelling.

Spacious Workout Area

Multi-floor training zones including a rooftop astroturf area with punching bags, battle ropes, and kettlebells under open skies.
